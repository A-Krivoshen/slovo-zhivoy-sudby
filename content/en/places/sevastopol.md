## Family context

In **1946**, while staying with the Krivosheins in Crimea, Tatyana Timofeevna’s party is invited to **Sevastopol** for Navy Day celebrations (the manuscript mentions Admiral Oktyabrsky inviting Dmitry Aleksandrovich).

This is not a sightseeing brochure — a **witness** episode: the city after the war.

{{< osm_map lat="44.6166" lon="33.5254" zoom="12" title="Sevastopol" caption="Harbour city; the notebook describes ruined houses, Malakhov Kurgan, the monument to the sunken ships." >}}

## What the manuscript records

- the city “completely smashed”; a few buildings, the quay, a water station still standing;  
- streets of “dead windows”;  
- **Malakhov Kurgan** — a sense of walking over the dead;  
- forts of the first defence at the bay entrance;  
- the **monument to the sunken ships**.

Sevastopol in this archive belongs to postwar **family Crimea**, not to a separate naval biography of the Samsonovs.
