# Beginning of the family

{{< archive_note type="identity" >}}
**Sasha** here is **[Aleksandr Dmitrievich Krivoshein](/en/people/aleksandr-krivoshein/)**, **husband** of Tatyana Timofeevna.
{{< /archive_note >}}

## Period

**Late 1940s** in the manuscript:

- decision to marry — after **Sasha (A. D. Krivoshein)**’s placement in Magnitogorsk and a trip to Simferopol;
- civil registration — **in Crimea** (text: Yalta / Gurzuf; no invented day);
- **1948** — birth of son **Dmitry (Mitya)**;
- then study, work, life in Moscow (see the family-and-children chapter).

Exact registry office calendar date is **not** published if not firmly in the text.
