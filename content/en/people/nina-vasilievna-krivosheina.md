**Nina Vasilievna Krivosheina** (née **Petrova**, **1907–1991**) is the **wife** of [Dmitry Aleksandrovich Krivoshein](/en/parents/dmitry-aleksandrovich-krivoshein/), the **mother** of [Aleksandr Dmitrievich Krivoshein](/en/people/aleksandr-krivoshein/) (Sasha), and the **mother-in-law** of [Tatyana Timofeevna Krivosheina](/en/people/tatyana-timofeevna-krivosheina/).

In Tatyana Timofeevna’s manuscript she appears as **“Nina Vasilievna”**, **“N. V.”**, **“Baba Nina”** (Crimea, household).

## Portrait: why this image

Identity is fixed by **chart context**, not by facial recognition:

1. On the [“Krivosheins” genealogy chart](/en/memoirs/diary/00-cover-and-charts/) (notebook p. **005**) the node is written **“Krivosheina (Petrova) Nina Vasilievna”**, **1907–1991**.  
2. The node is linked as **wife** of [Dmitry Aleksandrovich](/en/parents/dmitry-aleksandrovich-krivoshein/) and as mother of **“their son”** [Aleksandr Dmitrievich](/en/people/aleksandr-krivoshein/).  
3. The glued print sits **on that node** (nearby note “cemetery”).
