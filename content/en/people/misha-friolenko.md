## Brief note

**Friolenko Mikhail Makarovich** (**Misha**) — author of a two-page letter to [Tanya](/en/people/tatyana-timofeevna-krivosheina/) of **20 April 1943** (notebook leaves **128–129**).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name on the sheet** | Misha Friolenko; return address — **Friolenko Mikhail Makarovich** |
| **From** | Field post **18003 “g.”** |
| **Who** | a **comrade** (friend), **not** the brother |

In the letter text “your brother Gesha” is a **third person** — the author is **not** [Timofey Timofeevich Samsonov](/en/people/timofey-timofeevich-samsonov/) (Tyoma).

## Family links

No kinship on the published materials. The link is wartime correspondence and acquaintance: notebook context — a wounded comrade who visited when a regiment was reforming after Stalingrad (1943, ninth form).
