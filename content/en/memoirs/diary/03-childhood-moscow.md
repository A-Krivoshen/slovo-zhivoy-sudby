And so a new life began in the new

Papa was a great book lover; he  
and Mama collected a large library.  
When Papa was free on weekends

Papa treated books with great care and

There were always cats in our house. We children  
often quarreled over them. Each  
wanted to drag a cat into bed with them.  
Of course when we went away to the dacha, the  
cat was taken along, and the tom Lyubik  
hated cats; our cat he  
tolerated and even sometimes ate with her from the same  
bowl.

Papa and Mama loved cats too.  
Even when we had grown up, the  
parents still had a cat, Ksanka;  
when she had kittens, Papa took a large  
basin, laid all sorts of soft things in it  
and installed the whole feline family  
under his bed. And when the kittens  
grew up a little, Papa played with them, made  
them various little paper airplanes,  
and then Mama looked for good  
people to place  
a kitten with. With tears in her  
eyes she parted with a kitten.  
Ksanka (or Ksenia Zakharovna) was their  
last cat.

In the kitchen garden we also had to work:  
we weeded a little, watered a little; when  
the nights were cold and the cucumbers were small,  
we covered them for the night with news-

wanted to try them. But they were

And my brother and I crawled along the path
