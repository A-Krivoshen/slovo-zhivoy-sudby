## What this site is about

**Word of a Living Fate** is a family archive dedicated to the life of **Tatyana Timofeevna Krivosheina**, the story of her parents, family memory, photographs, documents, and the historical context of her time.

The site exists so living stories are preserved and can be passed on.

## What you will find

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Memoirs** | Memoirs and stories about Tatyana Timofeevna’s life |
| **Parents** | Her father and mother, family roots |
| **History** | Historical facts, dates, events, and period context |
| **Photos** | Family photo archive |
| **Documents** | Letters, certificates, official papers, and other sources |
