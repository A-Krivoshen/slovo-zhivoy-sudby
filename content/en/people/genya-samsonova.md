**Evgenia Timofeevna Samsonova** is the elder sister of **[Tatyana Timofeevna Krivosheina](/en/memoirs/about-tatyana-timofeevna/)** in the family notebook “Our genealogy: the Samsonovs.”

This site states **only what the manuscript and charts already say**. There is no separate external biography: gaps are **not** filled with general phrasing.

| **1936** | Verses / letter anchor in [ch. 10](/en/memoirs/diary/10-notebook-continued-080-089/) |

## Years (as in the sources)

From the **narrative** (pp. 087, 091):

- born in **Moscow in February 1920**;
- **died in 1941** (in the story — **October**, “she was 21”).

On the **family chart** (p. 003) the name carries **“1930–1941”** (as read from the chart). That range **does not match** “February 1920” and age 21 in 1941; the site does **not** resolve the discrepancy for the author — both source readings are recorded.

{{< archive_note type="privacy" >}}
Death in **wartime** (1941) is from the manuscript; this card does **not** expand medical or death detail beyond chapters 10–11. Full narrative is in the notebook.
{{< /archive_note >}}
