# School 1940–1941

## Period

**Pre-war years and the start of the war** — from the childhood/school chapters and “School and war.”  
In the narrative: end of 7th form, move to 8th; **22 June 1941** — announcement of war (family at the radio).  
Then school in evacuation and return to a Moscow class (including classmate Sasha Krivoshein).
