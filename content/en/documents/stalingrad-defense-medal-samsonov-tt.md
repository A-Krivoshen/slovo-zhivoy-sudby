## What it is

A certificate awarding the medal **“For the Defence of Stalingrad”** (*«За оборону Сталинграда»*).

The form names **Guards Sergeant Timofey Timofeevich Samsonov**.

## Not the father

This is Tatyana Timofeevna’s **brother** — **uncle Tyoma**, **Timofey Timofeevich Samsonov** (b. 1923).

It is **not** her father **Timofey Petrovich Samsonov** (1888–1955). The patronymic on the form is **Timofeevich**, not Petrovich.

## Decree and presentation

- **Decree** of the Presidium of the Supreme Soviet of the USSR: **22 December 1942** (as printed on the blank — the medal’s establishment / legal basis).
- **Presentation date** on the certificate: **23 September 1943**.
- **Blank number** (from the scan): **II No. 34769**.

If family memory has mentioned another presentation year (for example 1945), the public site follows the **date written on the form**. Any discrepancy is flagged for family review against the original.

## Unit

Presented by the commander of the **79th Guards Mortar Regiment** (blank also has “commander of the 79th GMR”).

Signature: **Guards Lieutenant Colonel** — **V. Popov** (reading from the scan; flourish).

## Text (from the photo)

> For our Soviet Motherland  
> CERTIFICATE  
> For participation in the heroic defence of STALINGRAD  
> Guards Sergeant Samsonov Timofey Timofeevich  
> By decree of the PRESIDIUM OF THE SUPREME SOVIET OF THE USSR of 22 December 1942 awarded the medal “FOR THE DEFENCE OF STALINGRAD”  
> On behalf of the PRESIDIUM OF THE SUPREME SOVIET OF THE USSR the medal “For the Defence of Stalingrad”  
> presented “23” September 1943  
> II No. 34769  
> COMMANDER OF THE 79th GUARDS MORTAR REGIMENT  
> Guards Lieutenant Colonel V. Popov
