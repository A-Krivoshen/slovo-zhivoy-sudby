Wartime **letters** inserted in Tatyana Timofeevna’s notebook or preserved beside her narrative: voices from the front and from hospital, addressed to **parents**, to **mother**, and to **Tanya**.

This is a **separate document collection**, not a continuation of the diary text. In the notebook the letters sit next to memoir pages; here each letter is treated as a source on its own: leaf scan, readable text, and a line-oriented manuscript reading.

Related: [Medal “For the Defence of Stalingrad” — T. T. Samsonov](/en/documents/stalingrad-defense-medal-samsonov-tt/) (brother **Tyoma** / **Timosha**).

## How to read

- **Reading** — continuous text for reading.  
- **Diplomatic transcription** — line by line as on the leaf; uncertain places marked **[?]**.  
- Dates and signatures only where they can be read with confidence on the scan; otherwise “needs verification.”  
- **Timosha / Tyoma** is brother [Timofey Timofeevich Samsonov](/en/people/timofey-timofeevich-samsonov/), **not** father [Timofey Petrovich Samsonov](/en/people/timofey-petrovich-samsonov/) ([biography](/en/parents/timofey-petrovich-samsonov/)).
