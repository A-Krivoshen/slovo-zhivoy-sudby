## Place name

In wartime papers and family memory the city is **Stalingrad**.  
Its present official name is **Volgograd** (renamed in 1961).  
This page keeps the **historical** name for 1942–1943 and the award; the modern name is noted in parentheses.

{{< osm_map lat="48.7080" lon="44.5133" zoom="11" title="Volgograd (historically Stalingrad)" caption="City landmark; for this archive the meaning is the 1942–1943 defence and a family award document." >}}

## Why it appears here

Not a tourist guide — a **family link**:

- Tatyana Timofeevna’s brother — **Timofey Timofeevich Samsonov** (Uncle Tyoma / Timosha), Guards sergeant;
- the notebook and a standalone document preserve the certificate for the medal **“For the Defence of Stalingrad”**;
- **not** to be confused with father **[Timofey Petrovich Samsonov](/en/parents/timofey-petrovich-samsonov/)** — the form uses the patronymic **Timofeevich**.
