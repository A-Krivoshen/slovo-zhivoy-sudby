**Dmitry Aleksandrovich Krivoshein** (1905, Kirsanov, Tambov province — 1979, Moscow) was a Soviet state official and agronomist; **father of** Tatyana Timofeevna’s husband, **Aleksandr Dmitrievich Krivoshein**.

In Tatyana Timofeevna’s family notebook he appears in the **Krivoshein** chart (1905–1979; buried at Lyuberetskoye cemetery — per the notebook).

## Brief biography

Born into a meshchane family (Kirsanov). Member of the VKP(b) from **1938**.  
Graduated from the **Leningrad Agricultural Institute** in **1930**.

Worked as agronomist and state-farm director (Orenburg area; Ivanovo industrial region; RKKA state farm).

| Years | Post |
| --- | --- |
| 1938–1939 | Deputy chair, Rostov regional executive committee (agriculture) |
| 1939–1943 | **People’s Commissar** of grain and livestock state farms of the **RSFSR**; collegium member, USSR People’s Commissariat of State Farms |
| 1943–1946 | Head of the Main Administration of Southern State Farms |
| 1946–1949 | **Chair of the Crimean regional executive committee** |
| Jun–Nov 1949 | Chair of the Amur regional executive committee |
| 1950–1953 | Director, “Lyuberetskie polya orosheniya” state farm (Moscow region) |
| 1953–1954 | Deputy director, Potato Institute |
| 1954–1959 | Director, “Varskie” state farm (Ryazan region) |
| 1959–1961 | Deputy director, Moscow livestock state-farm trust |
| from 1961 | Senior seed agronomist, Mossovet model farm |

In **August 1949** most of the Crimean obkom bureau was repressed; on **2 September 1949** his son **Aleksandr** was arrested. D. A.’s **autobiography** records conviction under **Art. 111** for **10 years**; **Tatyana Timofeevna’s notebook** records **Art. 58** and **10 years**. Details, the article conflict and the camp path are on **[Aleksandr Dmitrievich’s page](/en/people/aleksandr-krivoshein/)**. D. A. Krivoshein was removed from the Amur post; in 1950 he was at the disposal of the Central Committee.  
According to the father’s autobiography, the son was **fully rehabilitated** in 1954 and **reinstated at work**.

### Awards

- Order of **Lenin** (1939)  
- Order of the **Red Banner of Labour** (1945)
