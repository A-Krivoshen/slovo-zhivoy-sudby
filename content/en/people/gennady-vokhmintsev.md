# Gennady Vokhmintsev

## Brief note

Wartime letters to **Tanya / Tanyusha** signed **Gennady**. On the **18 November 1943** sheet a family heading names **Gennady Vokhmintsev**.

The shorter “Tanyusha!” note (letter 131) is published in the same group; year on that sheet needs checking where unmarked. Letters **132** and **133** (18 and 29 November 1943) are dated on the sheets.

He is a **correspondent / friend**, not a Samsonov sibling.
