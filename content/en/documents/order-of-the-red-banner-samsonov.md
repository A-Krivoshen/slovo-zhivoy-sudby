## What it is

A diploma of the **Russian SFSR** All-Russian Central Executive Committee awarding citizen **Timofey Petrovich Samsonov** the badge of the Order of the **Red Banner**, in the revolutionary-era formula of service to the socialist homeland (full wording on the blank).

## Signatures

- Chairman of VTsIK: **M. Kalinin**  
- Year on the form partly cut off: “19__” — **check against original**
