**Samsonova (née Morozova) Eva / Evdokia Konstantinovna** (1900–1981) is the **mother** of [Tatyana Timofeevna Krivosheina](/en/people/tatyana-timofeevna-krivosheina/) and **wife** of [Timofey Petrovich Samsonov](/en/people/timofey-petrovich-samsonov/).

The main source is her **daughter’s notebook** ([“My mother”](/en/memoirs/diary/01-my-mother/)). There is no separate external biography: the site publishes what the **notebook and charts** say.

## Origins (from the manuscript)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Birth** | **14 March 1900** (2 March O.S.), **Moscow** |
| **Father** | Morozov Konstantin Mikhailovich — from Griboevo, Klin uyezd; worker |
| **Mother** | Solovyova Vera Osipovna — brought to Moscow at 13 |
| **Death** | **1981**; buried at **Novodevichy** (chart / narrative) |

## Work

Typist — **Tsentropechat**, then the **Cheka secret department**; Dzerzhinsky episodes in the mother’s chapter and [ch. 05](/en/memoirs/diary/05-father-cheka-verses/). Exact service years are **not** reconstructed without personnel files.

## Family

- Husband — [T. P. Samsonov](/en/people/timofey-petrovich-samsonov/)
- Children (notebook): [Genya](/en/people/genya-samsonova/); [Timofey Timofeevich (Tyoma)](/en/people/timofey-timofeevich-samsonov/); [Tatyana](/en/people/tatyana-timofeevna-krivosheina/)
- Genya — **daughter of the father from his first marriage**; Eva **raised** her as her own

## War years and after

Addressee of her son’s front letters ([105–106](/en/documents/letters-from-the-front/letter-105-106-to-mother/), [107](/en/documents/letters-from-the-front/letter-107/)). Evacuation and household in Tatyana’s war chapters.

## Family memory of repression

In the [2011 KP interview](/en/documents/kp-house-on-the-embankment-2011/), the daughter reports that during the repressions her mother **listened in fear at night**, and in the morning the children saw sealed flats in the [House on the Embankment](/en/places/serafimovich-street/). That is **Tatyana’s voice about her mother**, not a standalone biography of Eva Konstantinovna and not a dossier on neighbours. In the notebook the climate of 1937–38 and disappearances around them appear in [ch. 13](/en/memoirs/diary/13-school-friends-109-119/); on her mother more broadly — [“My mother”](/en/memoirs/diary/01-my-mother/).
