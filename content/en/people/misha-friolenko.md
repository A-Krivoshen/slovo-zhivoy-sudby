# Mikhail Makarovich Friolenko

## Brief note

**Friolenko Mikhail Makarovich** — signs as **Misha Friolenko** on a two-page letter to Tanya dated **20 April 1943** (field post **18003**).

He is a **comrade / friend**, **not** brother [Timofey Timofeevich Samsonov](/en/people/timofey-timofeevich-samsonov/) (Tyoma). The letter itself mentions “your brother Gesha” as a third person.

Notebook introduction (Tatyana’s note): a wounded comrade who visited when a regiment was reforming after Stalingrad (1943, ninth form) — see [School and war](/en/memoirs/diary/14-war-evacuation-120-127/) (around the letter insert).
