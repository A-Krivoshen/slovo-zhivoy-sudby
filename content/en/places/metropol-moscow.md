## Place

The **Hotel Metropol** in central Moscow was used in the early Soviet years as the **1st House of Soviets** — housing for senior staff.

Per Tatyana Timofeevna’s manuscript: after a brief stay on Brestskaya Street the family was told to live in the **1st House of Soviets (Hotel Metropol)**. Later the hotel was cleared of residents; the family moved to Maly Vlasyevsky, then **Vozdvizhenka**.

## Why it appears here

- early **marriage** of T. P. Samsonov and Eva Konstantinovna;  
- stepmother’s first meeting with **Lelya** (daughter from the father’s first marriage);  
- nearby in time — births of **Timofey** (1923) and **Tatyana** (1926);  
- a link in the housing chain: Metropol → Arbat → Vozdvizhenka → [Serafimovich Street](/en/places/serafimovich-street/).
