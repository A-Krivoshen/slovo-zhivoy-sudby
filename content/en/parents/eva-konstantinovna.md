**Samsonova (née Morozova) Eva / Evdokia Konstantinovna** (1900–1981) is the mother of **Tatyana Timofeevna**.

Main facts come from her **daughter’s first-person notebook** (“Our genealogy: the Samsonovs”).

→ Full narrative: **[My mother](/en/memoirs/diary/01-my-mother/)**

In brief:

- b. **14 Mar 1900**, Moscow;  
- father K. M. Morozov (Griboevo, Klin district); mother V. O. Solovyova;  
- typist: Centropechat → **Cheka** secret department;  
- wife of **T. P. Samsonov**;  
- buried at **Novodevichy**.
