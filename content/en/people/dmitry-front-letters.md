# Dmitry (front letters)

## Brief note

On two published wartime letters the signature is **Dmitry**.

- **Surname** and **exact relationship** to the Samsonov family are **not established** for publication.
- Do **not** invent a surname.
- Do **not** merge with [Timofey Timofeevich](/en/people/timofey-timofeevich-samsonov/) (Timosha / Tyoma).
- Do **not** identify as father [Timofey Petrovich](/en/people/timofey-petrovich-samsonov/).

The letters address parents and sister Tanya — they sit in the family notebook, but the author identity remains open.
