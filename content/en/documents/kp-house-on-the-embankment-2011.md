## Why the family archive keeps this source

In 2011 [Tatyana Timofeevna](/en/people/tatyana-timofeevna-krivosheina/) was about **eighty-five**. Komsomolskaya Pravda published a long feature on the house built **in part** around a walk and conversation with her. She is not background colour: she is the **interlocutor** — courtyards, early-1930s childhood, neighbours, her father [Timofey Petrovich](/en/people/timofey-petrovich-samsonov/), how the building changed under repression, and family memory of her mother [Eva Konstantinovna](/en/people/eva-konstantinovna-samsonova/).

Three reasons it matters here:

1. **Independent print** — her voice is fixed not only in the notebook but in an external newspaper.
2. **Layers of time** — the manuscript narrates the 1930s from inside family chronicle; the 2011 interview is spoken memory decades later, already public.
3. **Echoes of the notebook** — move, settlement commission, courtyard and clubs, neighbours who disappeared — **overlap** with diary chapters, so the paper is read *beside* the manuscript, not instead of it.

The article **complements** [“Our genealogy: the Samsonovs”](/en/memoirs/diary/) but **does not replace** it and is **not** treated as an official archival file for offices, tenant lists, or every neighbour’s fate.

## What it supports (with confidence)

- **Fact of the 2011 publication** with Tatyana Timofeevna — *document_confirmed* (kp.ru metadata).
- **Childhood life in the house**, clubs, courtyards — *family_testimony_published*; **corroborated** by ch. 03 and 13.
- **Atmosphere under repression**, sealed apartments — *family_testimony_published*; echoes ch. 13.
- **Father on the settlement commission** and giving up part of the flat — *corroborated* (notebook + interview) as **family memory**, not a state-archive act.
- **Commission chair named in memory** — in **notebook ch. 03** and the interview; published here only as the **daughter’s recollection**, not as reconstructed administration.
- **Father’s office around 1931** — the archive has **two readings**: [ch. 02](/en/memoirs/diary/02-father-marriage-children/) uncertain “TsIK[?]…”, [ch. 03](/en/memoirs/diary/03-childhood-moscow/) “administrator of affairs of the Central Committee of the VKP(b)”; the paper tracks the **second**. We do **not** fix one wording as personnel truth without external archives.
