## Reading

Tanyusha!

Somehow I have long had no letters from you. I am not amused, and I remain in your debt.  
I am alive and well.

I wish you health; greetings to papa and mama  
with greetings —  
**Gennady.**

I await letters.

1 March “Happy spring!”

## Diplomatic transcription

```
Танюша!

Что-то уже давно
от тебя не получаю
писем. Мне не смешно
и остаюсь должником.
Я жив, здоров.

Желаю здоровья, привет
папе и маме с
приветом -
— Геннадий.

Жду писем.

1 марта «С весной!».
```

## Notes

- The name “Gennady” on the leaf is clear; identity with **Gennady Vokhmintsev** (heading on the 18 Nov 1943 letter) is **likely but not proven** by hand alone without verification.  
- Do **not** treat the year of neighbouring leaves as a fact for this leaf — only as a hypothesis to check.
