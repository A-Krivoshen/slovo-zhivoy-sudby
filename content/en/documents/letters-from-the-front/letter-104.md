## Reading

Only partially recoverable: the leaf is crumpled and torn; many lines break off.

28.VII.44  
Hello, dear parents.

I received your letter… (then breaks and unclear passages: mentions of letters, “I am living well,” do not worry, greetings to acquaintances).

Greetings to Marusya[?], Mashenka, Lyolya[?], Lida[?] and Nikolai.  
I kiss you. Dmitry
