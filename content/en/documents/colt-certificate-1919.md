## Text (from the photo)

On **16 January 1919**, comrade **T. P. Samsonov** is certified to have received for wear the personal **Colt** revolver No. **75350** of the chairman of the All-Russian Extraordinary Commission.

Signed: Cheka chairman **F. Dzerzhinsky**.

## Why it matters

A named paper tying Timofey Petrovich to **Dzerzhinsky** at a personal level — alongside the group photo “Dzerzhinsky — Belenky — papa.”
