Seven points from **Tatyana Timofeevna’s** notebook.  
Coordinates are modern (OSM); meaning comes from the manuscript.

## 1. 2nd Brestskaya — mother’s childhood home

“Two rooms on the ground floor… near Belorussky station. The house still stands.”

{{< osm_map lat="55.77098" lon="37.59061" zoom="15" title="2nd Brestskaya Street" caption="Area of the house where grandfather Morozov, grandmother and mother (Eva Konstantinovna) lived." >}}

## 2. Belorussky station → Lubyanka

Mother **ran to work** from Belorussky to the Lubyanka: no trams, winter, sled with potatoes.

{{< osm_map lat="55.77637" lon="37.58170" zoom="15" title="Belorussky railway station" caption="Starting point of mother’s walk to work." >}}

{{< osm_map lat="55.75926" lon="37.62625" zoom="15" title="Lubyanka Square" caption="Cheka secret department — mother’s job; here she met father, T. P. Samsonov." >}}

## 3. Vozdvizhenka — 4th House of Soviets

Childhood flat: 4th floor, five rooms; same building as Kalinin’s reception.

{{< osm_map lat="55.75265" lon="37.60782" zoom="16" title="Vozdvizhenka Street" caption="The Vozdvizhenka house (later 4th House of Soviets) — family apartment." >}}

## 4. Alexandrovsky Garden and the Kremlin

Walks in the garden; father sometimes took them into the Kremlin — “he had access.”

{{< osm_map lat="55.75240" lon="37.60868" zoom="16" title="Alexandrovsky Garden" caption="Childhood walks." >}}

{{< osm_map lat="55.75162" lon="37.61812" zoom="15" title="Moscow Kremlin" caption="“Father took us to the Kremlin.”." >}}

## 5. The “little dead end” by the Pushkin Museum

Winter sleds after mother’s work — the lane behind the museum.

{{< osm_map lat="55.74700" lon="37.60500" zoom="16" title="Pushkin Museum area" caption="Landmark for the “little dead end” — evening sled rides." >}}

## 6. Novodevichy

**Timofey Petrovich** (1955) and **Eva Konstantinovna** (1981) are buried here.

{{< osm_map lat="55.72457" lon="37.55462" zoom="15" title="Novodevichy Cemetery" caption="Family memory: Tatyana Timofeevna’s parents." >}}

## 7. Father’s birthplace — Mihăileni (Bessarabia)

The chart says T. P. Samsonov was born in Bessarabia, village **Mikhailyany**.  
Map point: modern Mihăileni (Moldova); may need document checks.

{{< osm_map lat="48.04086" lon="27.61611" zoom="13" title="Mihăileni" caption="Point from the notebook entry on father’s birth. Verify against papers." >}}
