# Crimea trips

## Period

**Summer 1946** — first detailed trip (after exams; Sasha and Tatyana with Sasha’s parents in Crimea).  
Later — holidays **1946/47** and further visits (including marriage registration and Mitya’s birth) — **by years in the manuscript**, without invented days.

## Places

In the narrative: Simferopol, Alushta, Gurzuf, Bakhchisarai, Chufut-Kale, Sudak, Sevastopol, Yalta, Massandra, and others.  
Dedicated place pages may appear under Places; for now the diary chapters are the source.
