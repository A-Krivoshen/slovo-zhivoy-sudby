## Why a History section

Memoirs and family stories sit next to **historical facts**: dates, places, and events of the country and region. This section helps us:

- clarify chronology;
- understand *the world* in which personal stories unfolded;
- separate family tradition from verifiable information;
- connect the lives of Tatyana Timofeevna and her parents to their era.

## How materials will be written

1. **Fact** — short and clear.
2. **Date / period** — as far as known.
3. **Source** — document, reference work, family account, or “needs verification”.
4. **Family link** — why it matters for the archive.

## Topic stubs

- places where the family lived;
- key years and events;
- daily life and customs of the time;
- family chronology (as details become clear).
