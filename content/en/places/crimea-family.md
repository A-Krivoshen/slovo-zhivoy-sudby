## What this place means in the archive

**Crimea** here is not a travel guide but **family geography** of the postwar years:

- Tatyana Timofeevna’s father-in-law, **[Dmitry Aleksandrovich Krivoshein](/en/parents/dmitry-aleksandrovich-krivoshein/)**, was chairman of the Crimean regional executive committee in **1946–1949**;
- in summer **1946** Tatyana is invited to the Krivosheins; with **Sasha** (Aleksandr Dmitrievich) she travels the peninsula;
- later — holidays in Simferopol, marriage registration, birth of son **Mitya** (1948) — events tied to the Krivoshein household in Crimea.
