# Meeting Sasha

## Period

**After return from evacuation**, senior school (9th form and later in the text), then youth in the **mid-1940s**.  
No calendar day of the first meeting is given — the manuscript describes unloading firewood at school and classmate **Sasha Krivoshein**.
