## Brief note

**Gennady Vokhmintsev** — name in a **family heading** on the letter of **18 Nov 1943** (leaf **132**); the signature on the letters is **Gennady**.

Letters are addressed to **Tanya / Tanyusha** — [Tatyana Timofeevna](/en/people/tatyana-timofeevna-krivosheina/).

| Leaf | Date | On the sheet |
|------|------|----------------|
| **132** | 18.11.1943 | heading “Letter from **Gennady Vokhmintsev**”; signature Gennady |
| **133** | 29.11.1943 | signature **Gennady** (continuation) |
| **131** | 1 March (year **not on the leaf**) | signature **Gennady**; full surname **needs verification** |

Identity of the author of leaf **131** with Vokhmintsev is **likely** (same first name, neighbouring leaves) **but not proven** by handwriting alone without a separate check.

## Family links

On the published letters he is a **correspondent**, not a Samsonov/Krivoshein family member. The text greets the addressee’s “papa and mama”.
