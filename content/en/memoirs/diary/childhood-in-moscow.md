## Home

First on **Maly Vlasievsky** Lane (Arbat), then on **Vozdvizhenka**.  
Later the building was called the **4th House of Soviets** (also home to **M. I. Kalinin’s** reception office).

Flat on the **4th floor**, windows onto Vozdvizhenka. **Five rooms**: nursery, grandmother’s room, bedroom, study, dining room; a large kitchen with a tiled wood stove — **no gas**.

## Walks

Walks in the **Alexandrovsky Garden**.  
Sometimes **father** took them into the **Kremlin** — “he had access.”  

Winter evenings after work **mother** pulled the children on sleds in the lane behind the Pushkin Museum — they called it the **“little dead end.”** No car; “we were all so happy together.”

## Tea

Home for tea. Mother: “Call the children for tea.”  
I ran down the corridor: **“Children, tea!”**

## Piano

Mother wanted the girls to learn music and bought a **piano on installment**.  
At father’s workplace they organized a club…
