## Place

**Serafimovich Street** in Moscow is the address of the **House of Government** (“House on the Embankment”).  
In **1931** the Samsonov family moves into this new building. The father sat on the settlement commission.

On public archive pages we name the **street and the historic house**, **without apartment or entrance numbers**.

{{< osm_map lat="55.7447" lon="37.6126" zoom="16" title="Serafimovich St. / House of Government" caption="Landmark of the House on the Embankment. Specific flats are not published on this site." >}}

## Why it appears here

- Tatyana Timofeevna’s **childhood and teenage home** after Vozdvizhenka;  
- **postal address** of her mother on 1943 front letters (“House of Government”);  
- tied to **[T. P. Samsonov](/en/parents/timofey-petrovich-samsonov/)** (work near the Central Executive Committee) and **[Eva Konstantinovna](/en/parents/eva-konstantinovna/)** (Party work in the city).

## Tatyana Timofeevna’s recollections

The family settled here when [Tatyana Timofeevna](/en/people/tatyana-timofeevna-krivosheina/) was a child (**1931**; about age five). Two layers of memory:

| **2011 interview** | spoken account for KP: early-1930s childhood atmosphere, the residential complex, repression, sealed apartments, family memory of father and mother | [source card](/en/documents/kp-house-on-the-embankment-2011/) · [KP original (Russian)](https://www.kp.ru/daily/25798.5/2780009/) |
