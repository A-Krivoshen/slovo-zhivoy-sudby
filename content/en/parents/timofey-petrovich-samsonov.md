## Who he is

**Timofey Petrovich Samsonov** is the father of **Tatyana Timofeevna Krivosheina**.  
According to the plaque on a family bust photograph: **1888–1955**.

On photo margins his daughter wrote *“my dad”* / *“papa”* and kept verses about him.

> Primary source for this draft: the Google Photos album “Самсонов Тимофей Петрович” (local copy: `inbox/scans/photos/parents-samsonovy/`).  
> Below is what can be read **from the images and papers themselves**. Details may be refined later.

## What the archive shows

### Portraits

- A young oval studio portrait (**Kapustinsky / Sheremetev, Moscow**), labeled *Samsonov Timofey Petrovich*, *my dad*.  
- Later portraits: at a writing desk; in a suit; an official print with a **Bashkir ASSR Supreme Soviet Presidium** stamp.  
- A bust: “Timofey Petrovich Samsonov, 1888–1955,” with handwritten *verses about papa* in a notebook below.

### Revolution, Cheka, documents

- A winter group of three men; margin note: **“F. E. Dzerzhinsky, Belenky, Papa.”**  
- Certificate dated **16 January 1919**: Cheka chairman **F. E. Dzerzhinsky** presents comrade **T. P. Samsonov** with his personal Colt revolver No. 75350.  
- **Order of the Red Banner** diploma (VTsIK) in the name of **Timofey Petrovich Samsonov**, signed by **M. Kalinin**.  
- A GPU collegium commendation / badge document for struggle against counter-revolution (per form text).  
- Clipping from ***Pravda*, 20 June 1929** — article “On Dzerzhinsky,” signed **SAMSONOV**.

### Siberia, 1910

Photo of the **Alexandrovsky Central** prison (Siberia).  
Family caption: *“Father was imprisoned here in the summer of 1910.”*

### Britain, 1917

British **Expulsion Order** (Aliens Act 1905):  
Liverpool Police Court (7 April 1917), Whitehall order of **28 September 1917**.  
Name forms: *Tomofi Sainsanov or Samsonov*, also *Michael Nemchinoff* — spellings need family/historical checking.

## Mother — Eva Konstantinovna

Family memory names Tatyana Timofeevna’s mother as **Eva Konstantinovna**.  
She is **not yet clearly identified** in this album (a couple on a bench is labeled as Dzerzhinsky with his wife).

A separate page will be added when labeled photos appear.
