# Meeting Sasha

{{< archive_note type="identity" >}}
**Sasha** in this event and throughout the family line of the notebook is **[Aleksandr Dmitrievich Krivoshein](/en/people/aleksandr-krivoshein/)** (1926–1988), **husband** of Tatyana Timofeevna.  
Do not confuse with other “Sashas” in the manuscript (e.g. a classmate surname in a transcript reading, or “Sasha sends a kiss” on wartime letter 107 — **not** the husband).
{{< /archive_note >}}

## Period

**After return from evacuation**, senior school (9th form and later in the text), then youth in the **mid-1940s**.  
No calendar day of the first meeting is given — the manuscript describes unloading firewood at school and classmate **Sasha Krivoshein** (= A. D. Krivoshein).
