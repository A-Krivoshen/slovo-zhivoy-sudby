# Defence of Stalingrad

## Period

**1942–1943** — defence period and medal dates from the certificate form:

- decree **22 December 1942** (as on the form);
- presentation date on the form: **23 September 1943**.

Day-by-day combat participation is **not** recorded on the form — we do not invent it.

## Places

- **Stalingrad** (historical name; now Volgograd) — in the award title and family context
- Moscow — family address and storage of the papers in the notebook

## Participants

- [Timofey Timofeevich Samsonov](/en/people/timofey-timofeevich-samsonov/) (**Tyoma**) — guards sergeant on the certificate
- **Not** [Timofey Petrovich Samsonov](/en/people/timofey-petrovich-samsonov/) (father)
- [Tatyana Timofeevna](/en/people/tatyana-timofeevna-krivosheina/) — preserved the form in the notebook
- [Misha Friolenko](/en/people/misha-friolenko/) — notebook note: regiment reforming after Stalingrad (1943)
