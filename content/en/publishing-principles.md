Short rules for what appears on this site. For visitors and living relatives, see also [Privacy](/en/privacy/).

## Sources

- The core is the **family archive**: manuscripts, photographs, letters, official papers.
- Diary text and photo captions rest on the **original** (notebook, form, seal) when possible, not on paraphrase alone.
- Editorial notes are kept distinct from the source: context, not invented memory.
- Third-party open sources (e.g. reference pages) are credited when used.

## Uncertainty

- Illegible or disputed passages are marked (**[?]**, “needs verification”), not polished into a guess.
- An unknown letter-writer surname is **not invented**; name forms (Timosha, Dmitry, Misha…) are **not merged** without evidence in the text.
- Dates and identifications that conflict stay **cautious** until a document or a clear manuscript statement confirms them.
- Links among people, places, and events in the public archive graph carry a confidence level (from “document-confirmed” to “uncertain”) — see the project relation vocabulary in the repository docs.

## Privacy

- For **living** people, public text usually gives only a name, relationship, and birth **year** (no day or month), if publication is appropriate at all.
- We do not deliberately publish phone numbers, e-mail addresses, precise modern homes, or document numbers of living people.
- Some notebook leaves are **withheld** from standalone pages or galleries (including 099–100; scans such as 155 and 157 may remain under `static` without public links).
- Requests to hide or correct material: [Privacy](/en/privacy/).

## What this site does not do

- It does not replace the archive with a sensational biography.
- It does not conflate different people with similar names (e.g. Timofey **Petrovich** vs Timofey **Timofeevich**).
- It does not claim that every manuscript page is published: release is **deliberate**, driven by sources and ethics.
