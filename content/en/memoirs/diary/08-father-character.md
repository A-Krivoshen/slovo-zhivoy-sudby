**Meetings with I. V. Stalin.**

It was at the end of 1918 and the beginning of 1919. The 3rd Army,  
where Father was head of the Special Department,  
was catastrophically retreating. And then to the town of Glazov,  
where the army headquarters was, arrived I. V. Stalin,  
F. E. Dzerzhinsky. Father was summoned to headquarters,  
where he became acquainted with F. E., who de-  
manded that a number of needed infor-  
mation be provided to him. There he also saw Stalin for the first time.  
(see Papa’s notebook)

**Meetings with F. E. Dzerzhinsky**

The first meeting took place in Glazov. From the very  
first meeting, as Father wrote, complete mutual understanding  
was established between him and F. E.  
Father worked with F. E. from 1919 until  
F. E.’s death itself in 1926. Father had to  
work with F. E. not only along the Cheka line,  
but in the People’s Commissariat of Railways, in the Supreme Council of the National Economy of the USSR. His method of  
work — burning! Besides that F. E. was  
strikingly honest, never bent his soul,
