## Brief note

**Dmitry** — the name in the signature on two front letters inserted in the notebook (leaves **103** and **104**).

| | |
|---|---|
| **What is known** | Signature **“Dmitry”**; address to parents (and to “little sister Tanya” in letter 103) |
| **Surname** | **not established** |
| **Family link** | **exact relationship not yet established** |

### What not to do

- Do **not** equate with **Timosha** (letters [105–106](/en/documents/letters-from-the-front/letter-105-106-to-mother/))  
- Do **not** identify with [Timofey Petrovich Samsonov](/en/people/timofey-petrovich-samsonov/) (the father)  
- Do **not** merge with other “Dmitrys” in the archive without separate evidence  

## Family links

Unknown. The letters address “dear parents” / “parents and little sister Tanya” in the family notebook pack — that is **not enough** to call the author a relative.
