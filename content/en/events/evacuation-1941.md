# Evacuation 1941

## Period

**1941** (outbreak of war; notebook insert on **16 October 1941** in Moscow) and the following months of evacuation / life in the east (**Kuybyshev** in the daughter’s account). Full family departure and return dates are **not** stated more precisely than the manuscript allows.

## Places

- [Family Moscow](/en/places/family-moscow/)
- **Kuybyshev** (now Samara) — evacuation city in Tatyana’s narrative (school, daily life, hunger)
- Return toward Moscow — passes and the road home (see the RU chapter “School and war”)
