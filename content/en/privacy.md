## About the project

**сжс.рф** (“Word of a Living Fate”) is a **non-commercial family archive**. Materials concern the memory of **Tatyana Timofeevna Krivosheina**, her parents and kin. It is not a commercial service or a media platform.

## What we publish

- manuscripts, photographs and documents from the family archive;
- transcriptions and careful editorial notes;
- for **living** people — as a rule only a name, relationship and birth **year** (no day or month), if publication is appropriate at all.

We do not deliberately publish: phone numbers, addresses, e-mail addresses or document numbers of living people.

## Technical data about visitors

The site may use **Yandex Metrika** (a counter) to understand how pages are opened. Metrika may collect technical data: page URL, device type, approximate region, cookie identifiers, etc. — under Yandex’s own terms.

**Yandex RTB** ad units may also be loaded (ads; loader and floor blocks). Their operation is governed by Yandex policies.

We do **not** run user registration and do **not** sell contact lists.

## Cookies and third-party scripts

When you open the site the browser may load Yandex scripts (Metrika, ads) and OpenStreetMap tiles (if a page has a map). These are ordinary external requests.

## Requests about personal data

If you find information about yourself or relatives that you consider inappropriate (especially about living people), write to the archive owner:

- owner’s site: [krivoshein.site](https://krivoshein.site)

Please include the page URL and, if possible, the text fragment. We will try to hide, correct or remove the disputed part promptly.

## Limitation

This page is a **practical** description of a family website, not legal advice and not an offer.
